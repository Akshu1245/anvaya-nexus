# Action Impact Preview Specification

## Priority boundary

- **P0:** working deterministic prioritisation engine and simple functional panel.
- **P1:** advanced visual polish, richer interaction, scenario comparison, and optional downloadable output.

## P0 purpose

Rank authorised verification activities by the material gaps they could resolve. The preview does not predict investigation success, conviction, guilt, or order coercive action.

## Formula

`Priority = Gap Severity × Questions Addressed × Time Sensitivity ÷ Estimated Effort`

Each input uses documented bounded ordinal values. Output shows the component values, dependencies, limitations, and resulting rank. Ties are resolved by higher time sensitivity, then lower effort, then stable action ID.

## Golden demonstration

**Review available CCTV** must rank highest because it tests both vehicle-colour and number-of-offenders discrepancies and is time-sensitive due to footage retention.

Other examples: verify IMEI source record; recheck witness description; broaden area search.

## Safety wording

Required label: **Suggested verification priority — not a legal direction, coercive instruction, or prediction of investigation success.**

Only authorised, non-coercive verification categories configured for the prototype may be ranked. The officer makes the final decision.
